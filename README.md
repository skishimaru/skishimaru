Hi! I'm Shelbie Ishimaru, and I am an aspiring ecological statistician.

- I am a PhD student at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa's [Hawaiʻi Institute of Marine Biology](https://www.himb.hawaii.edu/) within the [Marine Ecological Theory Lab](https://lmcmanus47.github.io/) 
- I am a graduate assistant at NOAA Pacific Island Fisheries Science Center's [Protected Species Division](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/pacific-islands/science-data/providing-science-protect-marine-species) within the [Marine Turtle Biology and Assessment Program](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/pacific-islands/science-data/sea-turtle-research-pacific)
- My doctoral research focuses on mark-recapture modeling to produce update abundance estimates and nesting demographic information for the Hawaiian green sea turtle (honu)
